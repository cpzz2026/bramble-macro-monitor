About the data. Market values (equities, FX, rates, energy, commodities, Tesco) are live from a public markets feed and refresh automatically on a schedule. Macro indicators — including consumer sentiment and CPI — come from FRED and populate once a FRED API key is configured; until then those tiles read "awaiting feed". Fertiliser (urea) and the global PMI have no free feed and remain placeholders. Scenario statuses are computed from live signal thresholds; the analogues and implications are an analytical framework for discussion, not investment advice or a forecast.
